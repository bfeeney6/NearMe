Our team built a very basic framework for our app idea called NearMe which is an app that allows people to stay up to date with everything happening in their locality. Our idea would be to allow people to set up a profile for their location and they could meet new people, check for local business offers, see any local news and organise/take part in local events. It removes the clunkiness of using multifunctional apps like Facebook and Instagram by having an app that has this sole purpose. Obviously we are still in an early design phase as our app still has limited functionality etc however, it provides a basic idea of how we intend to make the app.
